Top three activities to do in Tampere

Go for a picnic in the local park

The Hatanpaa arboretum is perfect for picnic, getting some tan, or just chilling out.

Relax in a traditional Finnish sauna

Experience the authentic Finnish sauna culture and unwind after a long day.

Enjoy delicious food in Kauppahalli

Kauppahalli is a great place to taste local delicacies and enjoy a variety of dishes.
